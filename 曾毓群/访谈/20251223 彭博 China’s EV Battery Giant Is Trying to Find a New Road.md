China’s EV Battery Giant Is Trying to Find a New Road

中国电动汽车电池巨头正探索新路径

CATL flooded the domestic market with inexpensive, efficient batteries. It wants to do the same overseas, but European politicians—and Donald Trump—stand in its way.

宁德时代（ CATL ）凭借价格低廉、性能高效的电池迅速占领了国内市场。如今，它希望在海外复制这一成功，但欧洲政界人士乃至唐纳德 · 特朗普却成了其前行路上的障碍。

Inside the world’s largest battery plant, delicate robot arms coat sheets of aluminum and copper foil—each only 5 micrometers thick, about a 20th the diameter of a human hair—with an electrode slurry, a process that resembles nothing so much as spreading jam on bread. The coated material, along with a thin separator film, is guided along by massive steel rollers, precisely calibrated to ensure each strip is neither too tight nor too loose. The next step is to wind the strips into tightly packed spirals known as jelly rolls and seal them into casings. More robotic arms then inject an electrolyte solution, turning the assemblage of metal and chemicals into a highly efficient lithium-ion battery.

在全球最大的电池工厂内，精密的机械臂将电极浆料均匀涂覆于铝箔与铜箔上 —— 每片箔材仅 5 微米厚，约为人类发丝直径的二十分之一 —— 整个过程宛如在面包上涂抹果酱般流畅。涂覆后的材料与一层超薄隔膜一同被巨型钢辊精准传送，辊轮经过精密校准，确保每条极片张力恰到好处。随后，这些极片被卷绕成紧密的螺旋结构（业内称为 “ 卷芯 ” ），并封装入壳体。更多机械臂随即注入电解液，使金属与化学物质的精密组合蜕变为高性能锂离子电池。

The process is largely automated, but a retinue of human inspectors trained to detect the slightest imperfection keeps watch at stations throughout the factory. A single air bubble or misaligned weld could lead to a short circuit, a rare glitch in production. Once inspected, the aluminum-enclosed battery packs will be loaded onto shelves to let stand for hours to days before being shipped to electric-vehicle factories around the world.

该流程高度自动化，但工厂各处仍布有训练有素的人工检测员，随时捕捉最细微的瑕疵。哪怕只是一个微小气泡或毫厘之差的焊接偏移，都可能引发短路 —— 尽管此类生产异常极为罕见。经检验合格的铝壳电池包会被置于货架静置数小时至数日，随后发往全球各地的电动汽车制造工厂。

This facility, covering almost 2 square miles in Ningde, in southeastern China, is designed to produce 60 gigawatt-hours of EV batteries annually, enough to power 1 million Model Ys for Tesla Inc. It’s the flagship manufacturing hub for one of the world’s most important automotive companies, Contemporary Amperex Technology Co. Ltd., or CATL. More than 1 out of every 3 EVs made this year had a CATL battery inside, according to data from South Korea-based SNE Research—including cars from BMW, Ford, Honda, Mercedes and Tesla, as well as Chinese brands such as Xiaomi. In May the company raised $5.3 billion by selling shares in Hong Kong, and its 57-year-old founder, Yuqun “Robin” Zeng, is now one of the 30 wealthiest people on the planet, according to the Bloomberg Billionaires Index, with an estimated fortune of $58.3 billion.

这座位于中国东南部宁德市的工厂占地近 2 平方英里，设计年产能达 60 吉瓦时电动汽车电池，足以为特斯拉 100 万辆 Model Y 提供动力。这里是全球最重要的汽车供应链企业之一 —— 宁德时代新能源科技股份有限公司（简称 CATL ）的旗舰生产基地。据韩国 SNE Research 数据显示，今年全球每三辆新生产的电动汽车中，就有一辆以上搭载 CATL 电池，客户涵盖宝马、福特、本田、梅赛德斯 \- 奔驰、特斯拉以及小米等中国品牌。今年 5 月，公司通过港股发行股票融资 53 亿美元。其 57 岁创始人曾毓群现已成为全球前 30 大富豪之一，据彭博亿万富翁指数估算，其个人财富约 583 亿美元。

But for CATL, as with other companies that come to lead their industries, unique success is creating unique challenges. Growth is slowing in the Chinese EV market, the world’s largest, where more than 50% of new passenger cars sold are either fully electric or plug-in hybrids. BloombergNEF forecasts the country will soon have a surplus of battery manufacturing, with predictable effects on prices. CATL is setting up factories in Europe and Southeast Asia, regions where it hopes to build businesses as successful as the one at home. Zeng has an eye on North America too. But his company has been discouraged from manufacturing in the US by various government policies, and President Donald Trump’s administration has maintained a ban that prevents it from bidding on government contracts—all while gutting measures designed to encourage EV adoption. (Ford Motor Co., which licenses technology from CATL in the US, recently said it would take $19.5 billion in charges related to its money-losing electric operations.) Meanwhile, even in more EV-friendly Europe, governments are growing wary of depending on Chinese companies for batteries and other critical technologies.

然而，对宁德时代而言，如同其他行业领军企业一样，非凡的成功也带来了独特的挑战。全球最大的电动汽车市场 —— 中国，其增长正在放缓：新售乘用车中超过半数已是纯电动或插电混动车型。彭博新能源财经（ BloombergNEF ）预测，中国电池制造产能很快将出现过剩，价格下行压力随之而来。宁德时代正积极在欧洲和东南亚建厂，期望在这些地区复制其国内的成功模式。创始人曾毓群也在密切关注北美市场。但受美国多项政策限制，公司在美设厂受阻；特朗普政府维持禁令，禁止其参与联邦政府采购竞标 —— 与此同时，还大幅削减了鼓励电动汽车普及的激励措施。（已获宁德时代技术授权的福特汽车近期宣布，其亏损的电动车业务将计提 195 亿美元减值损失。）即便在电动汽车政策更友好的欧洲，各国政府也开始对在电池等关键技术领域过度依赖中国企业日益警惕。

CATL is therefore treading a fine line, between being perceived as an essential innovator and an ominous threat. In an interview with Bloomberg Businessweek, Zeng says he intends to push forward regardless, pumping out more and better batteries and pushing CATL’s engineers to develop more ways for customers to use them. “Blindly chasing geopolitical changes would essentially cripple our operations,” he says. “That’s why having a Plan B is a core part of our strategy.” Having built an engine of unprecedented scale to electrify global transportation, in other words, Zeng has no choice but to run it ever faster and further.

因此，宁德时代正行走在一条微妙的边界线上：既要被视为不可或缺的创新者，又要避免被当作潜在威胁。在接受《彭博商业周刊》采访时，曾毓群表示，无论外部环境如何变化，他都将坚定推进，持续生产更多、更好的电池，并推动工程师开发更多应用场景。 “ 盲目追随地缘政治变动，本质上会拖垮我们的运营， ” 他说， “ 这就是为什么 ‘B 计划 ’ 是我们战略的核心。 ” 换言之，既然已打造出一台史无前例、驱动全球交通电气化的引擎，曾毓群除了让它加速运转、走得更远之外，别无选择。

Zeng’s entrepreneurial story has a familiar ring in China. He was born in 1968, and came of age in the 1980s, as the economy began to open to private enterprise. Like millions of ambitious students of his generation, he studied engineering in college, then spent a few unremarkable months at a state-owned company in his home province of Fujian. He soon tired of the routine and moved, in 1989, to Guangdong, where pro-market reforms were proceeding much faster than elsewhere.

曾毓群的创业故事在中国颇具代表性。他生于 1968 年，成长于八十年代 —— 那是中国经济向私营企业开放的起点。与同代千百万怀揣抱负的学子一样，他大学主修工程专业，毕业后在福建老家一家国企工作了短短数月，经历平淡。他很快厌倦了这种按部就班的生活，于 1989 年南下广东，那里的市场化改革步伐远快于全国其他地区。

Zeng found work with a unit of TDK Corp., a Japanese manufacturer of electronic components. Ten years later he left to co-found a startup, Amperex Technology Co. Ltd., to develop batteries for various small gadgets. Using a license to a Bell Labs patent, Zeng ironed out technical glitches—such as a dangerous tendency for power packs to swell after being charged a few times—and later won a contract with Apple Inc. to supply batteries for the iPod. Deals with other electronics giants followed, and in 2005 Zeng sold his venture back to TDK, in an acquisition Chinese media reported was worth $100 million.

曾毓群最初在日本电子元件制造商 TDK 集团旗下部门工作。十年后，他离职联合创立初创企业 Amperex Technology （ ATL ），专注于为各类小型设备开发电池。通过获得贝尔实验室的一项专利授权，他攻克了多项技术难题，例如电池反复充电后易鼓包的安全隐患。此后，公司成功赢得苹果订单，成为 iPod 的电池供应商。随着与其他电子巨头合作的展开，业务不断拓展。 2005 年，曾毓群将公司回售给 TDK ，据中国媒体报道，交易金额约 1 亿美元。

After working for his old employer for six more years, Zeng again struck out on his own, creating a company that would focus on the nascent electric-car industry. (Tesla introduced the Model S, arguably the first mass-market EV, in 2012.) For an English name, Zeng simply added “Contemporary” to that of his first startup. In Chinese he called it “Ningde Era”—in honor of his hometown, where it would be based. CATL’s first real break came in the form of a supply agreement with BMW AG, which established its bona fides in the automotive supply chain. “We were very fortunate to work with BMW since our earliest days,” says Matt Shen, CATL’s general manager for Europe. “We learned incredibly detailed and rigorous requirements from them.”

在前雇主处又工作六年后，曾毓群再度创业，成立了一家专注新兴电动汽车产业的公司。（特斯拉于 2012 年推出 Model S ，这被视为首款真正面向大众市场的电动汽车。）公司的英文名，他直接在首次创业公司名前加上 “Contemporary” ；中文则取名 “ 宁德时代 ” ，既致敬家乡，也将总部设于此地。宁德时代的首个重大突破，是与宝马集团达成供应协议，为其在汽车供应链中赢得关键信誉背书。宁德时代欧洲区总经理沈昱表示： “ 我们非常幸运，自公司初创期就能与宝马合作。从他们那里，我们学到了极其细致和严谨的标准。 ”

The deal turned out to be a beneficial move for BMW too, giving it a head start on complying with rules requiring EVs sold in China to use domestic batteries to qualify for purchase subsidies. Those policies, which began to be implemented in 2015, were part of a larger plan to reduce dependence on imported components, and gave CATL a key advantage over bigger rivals such as South Korea’s LG Chem Ltd. and Japan’s Panasonic Corp. Soon it was landing supply contracts with the Chinese joint ventures of General Motors, Mercedes and Volkswagen.

事实证明，这笔交易对宝马同样有利，使其在满足中国政策要求上抢占先机 —— 当时规定，只有使用国产电池的电动车才能享受购车补贴。该政策于 2015 年实施，是降低进口零部件依赖整体战略的一部分，为宁德时代带来关键优势，助其超越 LG 化学、松下等规模更大的海外对手。不久后，公司便陆续拿下通用、梅赛德斯 \- 奔驰和大众在华合资企业的电池订单。

CATL first went public on the Shenzhen exchange in 2018, giving Zeng a paper net worth of $3.4 billion. It then won business from Elon Musk, who was looking to equip Tesla models made at a new plant in Shanghai. In 2020, CATL began supplying power packs for Shanghai-built Model 3s; the deal was later extended to Model Ys, and to include vehicles exported from China to other countries. (For its non-Chinese production, Tesla primarily sources from Panasonic and LG.) In the fast-growing domestic EV market, CATL had become so successful that some carmakers complained. “We’re all working for CATL now,” Zeng Qinghong, then the chairman of Guangzhou Automobile Group Co. (GAC), grumbled when the company hiked prices in 2022, amid rising material costs.

宁德时代于 2018 年在深交所上市，曾毓群账面身家由此达到 34 亿美元。随后，公司赢得埃隆 · 马斯克的订单，为其上海新工厂生产的特斯拉车型提供电池。 2020 年，宁德时代开始为上海产 Model 3 供应电池包；协议后来扩展至 Model Y ，并涵盖从中国出口至其他国家的车辆。（对于非中国产车型，特斯拉主要从松下和 LG 采购。）在国内迅猛扩张的电动车市场，宁德时代取得巨大成功，甚至引发车企抱怨。 2022 年，在原材料成本飙升背景下提价后，时任广汽集团董事长曾庆洪曾发牢骚： “ 我们现在都是在给宁德时代打工。 ”

One effect of this success was the transformation of Ningde, previously among the poorest cities in eastern China. Today it teems with car and ship traffic generated by CATL, which is by far the largest local employer and taxpayer. Compared with the bright lights of Shanghai or Shenzhen, the city is still a backwater, and attracting talented staff is a perennial challenge. To draw them, CATL offers perks such as subsidized housing and educational support, as well as basketball courts, gyms and other recreational facilities. It also tries to foster community, to an extent that can raise outsiders’ eyebrows: Single employees can enroll in a corporate matchmaking service, and those who pair off can celebrate their nuptials in CATL-organized group weddings.

这一成功深刻改变了宁德 —— 这座曾是中国东部最贫困城市之一的地方。如今，因宁德时代而生的车流船运熙来攘往。作为当地迄今最大的雇主和纳税人，公司重塑了城市面貌。与上海、深圳的繁华相比，宁德仍显偏僻，吸引优秀人才始终是一大挑战。为此，公司提供住房补贴、子女教育支持等福利，并配备篮球场、健身房等休闲设施。它甚至着力营造社区氛围，其程度有时令外人颇感意外：单身员工可报名参加公司内部婚恋配对服务，成功牵手的伴侣还能在宁德时代举办的集体婚礼中喜结连理。

Critics note that, like other Chinese industrial giants, CATL has benefited from generous subsidies as well as protectionist policies that allowed it to become established with limited foreign competition. And it isn’t considered especially unique in its mastery of the chemistry of lithium-ion batteries, or of an increasingly popular alternative, lithium-iron-phosphate—at least not compared with Japanese and Korean peers. It’s working on novel approaches such as solid-state batteries, which replace the liquid electrolyte in today’s lithium-ion units with a solid separator and promise monumental performance improvements if technical challenges can be overcome. But so are CATL’s competitors.

批评人士指出，宁德时代与其他中国工业巨头一样，受益于政府慷慨补贴和保护主义政策，使其能在有限的外国竞争环境中站稳脚跟。此外，在锂离子电池（或其日益流行的替代品磷酸铁锂电池）的化学技术领域，宁德时代并不被认为具备特别的独特性 —— 至少与日韩同行相比是如此。公司正研发固态电池等新技术，这类电池用固态隔膜取代现有液态电解质，若能攻克技术难关，有望带来性能飞跃。不过，其竞争对手也在同步推进类似研发。

What sets it apart, instead, are efficiencies on the production line. Among producers, “the fundamental chemistries aren’t that different. What is different is how inexpensively they can be manufactured,” says Gill Pratt, the chief scientist of Toyota Motor Corp. “It really is much more about manufacturing and production than it is about some huge science breakthrough.”

真正使其脱颖而出的，是生产线上的效率。丰田汽车首席科学家吉尔 · 普拉特（ Gill Pratt ）指出： “ 各家生产商的基础化学成分其实相差不大，真正的区别在于能以多低的成本进行制造。这更多关乎制造工艺与生产效率，而非重大的科学突破。 ”

CATL’s industrial prowess has allowed it to effectively flood the Chinese market, forcing it to look for new applications to soak up its growing production. But even the most successful expansion in China won’t be enough to keep the company moving at the breakneck pace Zeng envisions. For that it will need new markets.

宁德时代强大的产业实力使其几乎主导了国内市场，这迫使它必须寻找新应用场景以消化不断增长的产能。然而，即使在中国市场取得再大成功，也不足以支撑公司按曾设想的极速步伐持续发展。因此，开拓新市场势在必行。

CATL’s first-ever overseas factory, inaugurated three years ago, is in Germany’s heartland, not far from the medieval city of Erfurt. In contrast to the crowded facilities in Ningde, the German plant is airy and open, its shop floor and offices bathed in natural light. It’s proved to be an expensive experiment. The complex was designed to produce about 14 gigawatt-hours of batteries per year, enough to power as many as 350,000 vehicles. While CATL declines to quantify its current production volume, the figure is clearly far lower.

三年前，宁德时代首座海外工厂在德国中部投产，距古城爱尔福特不远。与宁德总部拥挤的厂房不同，这座德国工厂空间开阔、光线充足，车间与办公室均沐浴在自然光中。然而，这被证明是一次成本高昂的尝试。该工厂原设计年产能约 14 吉瓦时，足以驱动多达 35 万辆电动车。尽管宁德时代未公布当前实际产量，但显然远未达设计水平。

Company officials point to several reasons for the slow start, including language barriers between local hires and Chinese expatriates, who initially accounted for more than a third of staff. (The proportion is now below 10%, out of about 2,000 employees.) Many of those who applied for jobs were from Eastern Europe rather than Germany, and at first, CATL didn’t have the right interpreters on hand. Conversations had to be translated from Chinese to German, then to Czech or Polish and back again—a cumbersome process, especially when dealing with complex technical matters. Another problem: the gap between Chinese working culture, with its emphasis on long hours, and the more languid expectations of European staff.

公司高管指出了起步缓慢的几个原因，包括本地员工与中国外派人员之间的语言障碍，后者初期曾占员工总数三分之一以上。（目前该比例已降至 10% 左右，员工总数约 2000 人。）许多求职者来自东欧而非德国，而宁德时代起初并未配备合适的口译人员。交流需先从中文译成德语，再转为捷克语或波兰语，然后回译 —— 这一过程繁琐低效，处理复杂技术问题时尤为不便。另一个问题是：强调长时间工作的中国企业文化与欧洲员工相对宽松的工作预期之间存在显著差异。

The upshot is that expenses at the German operations, whether to meet regulatory requirements or hire enough trained employees, are much higher than in China, and batteries produced there are priced accordingly. The terms of CATL’s supply deals with automakers are confidential, and the cost paid can vary widely depending on the size of orders and their precise composition. But according to people familiar with the matter, who asked not to be identified describing private deliberations, some automakers have requested that CATL fill their orders with a mix of imported and European-produced units to keep costs stable. As a result of this preference and sluggish production, the majority of CATL batteries used in European-made EVs still come from China, the people say.

结果是，德国工厂的运营成本 —— 无论是满足法规要求还是招聘足额合格员工 —— 都远高于中国，因此当地生产的电池价格也水涨船高。虽然宁德时代与车企的供货条款保密，实际成本会因订单规模和具体构成而有显著差异，但据不愿具名的知情人士透露，一些车企为稳定成本，已要求宁德时代采用进口与欧洲本地生产相结合的电池来履行订单。正因为这种偏好以及当地产能爬坡缓慢，上述人士指出，目前欧洲产电动车所用的宁德时代电池，大部分仍源自中国。

For Zeng this sets up a dilemma. While growth in EV adoption in Europe has slowed, the region has embraced electrification faster than most, with the European Union legally bound to reach net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by the middle of the century. National governments, increasingly resistant to what they see as an overwhelming volume of cheap Chinese products, have pressured Brussels to reconsider aspects of this strategy, and in mid-December policymakers pulled back from plans to effectively ban new gasoline- and diesel-powered cars by 2035. The change followed warnings from automakers that the timeline could only be met by relying on China.

这对曾毓群而言构成了一个两难局面。尽管欧洲电动车普及速度有所放缓，但该地区实现电气化的进程总体上仍快于全球多数地区，且欧盟已立法承诺在本世纪中叶实现温室气体净零排放。然而，各国政府日益抵制他们认为如潮水般涌入的廉价中国商品，并已向布鲁塞尔施压，要求重新评估相关策略的某些环节。十二月中旬，政策制定者因此调整了原计划，不再坚持在 2035 年前实质禁售新的汽油车和柴油车。这一政策转向，源于汽车制造商发出警告：若想按期达标，只能依赖中国供应链。

As a result, CATL may have no alternative but to keep expanding in Europe and look for ways to contain costs as it does so. Batteries made in the EU may well remain more expensive, but they won’t risk provoking a protectionist response. “Given the looming risk of antisubsidy or antidumping measures nowadays, by fully manufacturing in the European Union you are probably less prone to such measures,” says Kai Kim, a lawyer who advises Chinese and European companies at Taylor Wessing in Shanghai.

因此，宁德时代可能别无选择，只能继续在欧洲扩张，同时设法控制扩张过程中的成本。虽然欧盟本土生产的电池可能仍会价格更高，但可以避免引发保护主义措施的风险。在上海泰勒 · 韦辛律师事务所为中国和欧洲企业提供咨询的律师金开指出： “ 考虑到当前反补贴或反倾销措施的风险日益临近，如果完全在欧盟境内生产，企业遭受此类措施影响的可能性可能会更低。 ”

CATL will soon begin production at its second European site, in Debrecen, Hungary—a €7 billion ($8.2 billion) project that will supply BMW, Mercedes-Benz Group AG and other carmakers. Ni Jun, CATL’s chief manufacturing officer, says it will incorporate lessons learned in Germany, employing a flexible layout that allows operations to be revamped depending on customer requirements. There are also plans for technicians to fly in from China and resolve problems as production ramps up, reaching from 30GWh to 40GWh in the first phase and eventually 100GWh or more. Another plant is under construction in Zaragoza, Spain—this one a joint venture with Stellantis NV, giving a major European industrial player a direct stake in CATL’s success. “We’re trying to make sure all the excessive mistakes we made in the previous production site will be corrected,” Ni says.

宁德时代很快将在其第二个欧洲生产基地 —— 匈牙利德布勒森启动生产。这个价值 70 亿欧元（约 82 亿美元）的项目，将为宝马、梅赛德斯 \- 奔驰集团等汽车制造商供货。宁德时代首席制造官倪军表示，该工厂将借鉴其在德国工厂的经验，采用灵活布局，以便根据客户需求调整产线运营。公司还计划从中国派遣技术人员常驻，在产能爬坡阶段解决问题。该基地一期产能规划为 30 至 40 吉瓦时，并计划最终提升至 100 吉瓦时或更高。此外，宁德时代与 Stellantis 集团在西班牙萨拉戈萨合资建设的另一座工厂也在施工中，此举使一家欧洲主流工业巨头直接参与了宁德时代的业务发展。倪军称： “ 我们正致力于确保纠正此前生产基地所犯的所有显著失误。 ”

Not long after ringing the bell at the Hong Kong Stock Exchange to celebrate CATL’s secondary listing, and having refreshed himself with a brief nap, Zeng sits down in a hotel conference room and lights up a slim cigarette. He’s obviously tired, with dark circles under his eyes, but he grows animated as he tells a Businessweek reporter about his plans for the next stage of CATL’s growth.

于 港交 所敲钟庆祝宁德时代二次上市后不久，曾毓群在酒店会议室坐下，点燃一支细烟。刚小憩片刻的他，眼带倦意与黑晕，但在向 本刊 记者描绘公司下一阶段发展蓝图时，却显得神采飞扬。

With the limits of its current business model becoming visible, at least in China, Zeng is eager to emphasize that the applications for its products could be much broader, with the right regulatory incentives. “The energy revolution will last for a long time, far beyond the target of carbon neutrality,” he says in Fujian-accented Mandarin. “The true, enormous future lies ahead in zero-carbon technologies.”

鉴于当前商业模式的局限性日益显现，至少在中国市场如此，曾先生迫切希望强调，在恰当的监管激励下，其产品的应用潜力可以更为广阔。他操着一口福建口音的普通话说： “ 能源革命将持续很长一段时间，其深远意义远超碳中和这一目标。真正的、巨大的未来在于零碳技术领域。 ”

In the near term, Zeng is focusing on more attainable goals, such as trying to convert holdouts among Chinese car buyers by offering battery-swapping technology, whereby depleted batteries are simply replaced rather than recharged. While traditional EV charging requires a potentially frustrating wait, a swap can be accomplished in about the time it takes to fill up a gas tank, assuming a driver’s car is compatible and the necessary infrastructure is in place. CATL calls its solution the “Choco-Swap,” because the batteries resemble giant candy bars.

The technology could make EV ownership more financially compelling. Instead of paying for the battery as part of the upfront cost of a car, drivers will be charged a monthly subscription fee that allows them to use Choco-Swap kiosks, 1,000 of which will soon be open in various Chinese cities. CATL has signed up major domestic carmakers to build dedicated models, including BAIC, Chery Automobile and FAW, though most of the interest has been for taxis and ride-hailing vehicles so far. It’s also teamed with e-commerce giant JD.com and GAC to build an electric hatchback that will sell for as little as 49,900 yuan ($7,086)—provided the customer also signs up for a battery plan.

这项技术有望提升电动汽车的经济性。消费者无需一次性支付包含电池的整车费用，改为按月订阅服务，即可使用即将在全国多个城市开设的 1000 个 Choco-Swap 换电柜。宁德时代已与北汽、奇瑞、一汽等国内主要车企达成合作，为其生产适配换电模式的专属车型，不过目前需求主要来自出租车和网约车领域。此外，宁德时代还与京东、广汽集团联手推出电动掀背车，若用户同时购买电池服务套餐，车辆起售价可低至 49,900 元（约合 7,086 美元）。

But Zeng is far from the first to try to build such a network, or to offer a subscription model. Several attempts have been made in and outside China, with limited success. (Tesla for a short time considered trying it in the US.) The logistics of ensuring batteries are always available at swap stations, high operating costs and the tiny number of swap-ready car models have all proved to be major problems. Although CATL may be better positioned to solve them than most, the plan still carries significant technical and financial risks.

然而，曾 总 并非尝试构建此类网络或推出 换电 模式的第一人。类似的尝试在中国国内外均有先例，但成效甚微。（例如，特斯拉曾一度考虑在美国推行此模式。）实际运营中，如何确保换电站电池持续供应、高昂的运维成本以及支持换电的车型稀少，都构成了主要障碍。尽管宁德时代在解决这些难题上可能具备比多数竞争者更有利的条件，但该计划依然面临显著的技术与财务风险。

Moreover, given CATL’s vast scale—its 2024 revenue was more than $50 billion—it’s unclear whether even a wildly successful swapping business would make a significant financial difference. The same is true of other new business lines: for example, helping municipalities redesign energy infrastructure and working with manufacturers to build zero-carbon factories. (One early client is Kweichow Moutai Co., the distiller of a popular, stomach-searing spirit.) Zeng insists these disparate initiatives will add up to a durable strategy over time. “We have grand visions,” he says, “but our progress will be steady and deliberate, not a rash jump.”

此外，考虑到宁德时代庞大的体量 —— 其 2024 年营收已超 500 亿美元 —— 即便是极为成功的换电业务，能否对其财务状况产生显著影响，目前尚不明朗。其他新业务线也是如此，例如：协助市政部门重新设计能源基础设施，以及帮助制造商打造零碳工厂（其早期客户之一便是生产广受欢迎但酒性浓烈的白酒企业 —— 贵州茅台）。董事长曾毓群坚信，这些多元化的举措假以时日将共同构成一个稳健的长远战略。 “ 我们怀有远大抱负， ” 他表示， “ 但我们的步伐将是稳健而审慎的，不会贸然跃进。 ”

For the foreseeable future, however, CATL’s core business will remain unchanged. Like many consumer-electronics products, EV batteries are prone to boom-and-bust cycles. Success requires huge upfront investments in capacity to create economies of scale. The payoff is supposed to come over time, with declining marginal costs for every battery produced. But eventually supply grows to exceed demand, forcing manufacturers to cut prices and potentially pushing them to the financial brink. The only way off the ride, particularly for a company of CATL’s size, is to find ways to sell more and different battery products, in ever more markets.

然而，在可预见的未来，宁德时代的核心业务将保持稳定。与许多消费电子产品类似，电动汽车电池行业容易经历盛衰周期。企业成功需要前期对产能进行巨额投资，以实现规模经济。其回报将随时间显现，随着产量增加，每块电池的边际成本会逐步降低。然而，供应最终可能超过需求，迫使制造商降价，甚至将其推向财务危机的边缘。要摆脱这种循环，尤其对于宁德时代这样规模的企业而言，唯一途径是在更广阔的市场中，销售更多样化、更大量的电池产品。

In the last weeks of President Joe Biden’s term, his administration placed CATL on a blacklist that bars it from working with the Pentagon and other federal agencies, citing the risk of security breaches. It remains on the list today. Meanwhile, congressional Republicans have launched a probe into Ford’s licensing of CATL’s technology. Some on Capitol Hill even tried to dissuade Bank of America Corp. and JPMorgan Chase & Co. from underwriting its Hong Kong share offering, citing ominous but vague national security concerns.

在乔 · 拜登总统任期即将结束的最后几周，其政府将宁德时代列入黑名单，以存在安全风险为由，禁止其与五角大楼等联邦机构进行合作。该公司至今仍位列该名单之上。与此同时，国会共和党人已就福特公司获取宁德时代技术授权一事启动调查。国会山的一些人士甚至试图阻止美国银行与摩根大通承销其在香港的股票发行，声称其涉及模糊不清且令人担忧的国家安全问题。

Both banks worked on the sale anyway; Ford, too, was undeterred, and it’s starting a business selling CATL-derived energy storage systems, essentially stationary batteries for facilities such as data centers, that it will manufacture in Kentucky and Michigan. (The move is an attempt to repurpose battery-production capacity as American EV demand plummets for the time being.) Meanwhile, Tesla is building lithium-iron-phosphate production lines in Nevada using CATL technology. But other US companies have distanced themselves. Duke Energy Corp., a major electricity provider for the South and Midwest, decided in 2023 to phase out CATL equipment in its energy storage operations. That followed pressure from lawmakers including Marco Rubio, then a senator from Florida, who said they were worried the technology could threaten the power grid.

两家银行依然促成了这笔交易；福特公司同样没有退缩，并正在启动一项新业务，销售采用宁德时代技术的储能系统。该系统本质上是用于数据中心等场所的固定式电池，将在肯塔基州和密歇根州生产。（此举旨在在美国电动汽车需求暂时骤降的背景下，重新调整电池产能的用途。）与此同时，特斯拉正利用宁德时代的技术，在内华达州建设磷酸铁锂生产线。然而，其他美国公司则选择了与宁德时代保持距离。美国南方和中西部地区的主要电力供应商 —— 杜克能源公司，已于 2023 年决定在其储能业务中逐步淘汰宁德时代的设备。这一决定源于包括当时来自佛罗里达州的参议员马尔科 · 卢比奥在内的议员所施加的压力，他们曾表示担忧该技术可能对电网安全构成威胁。

CATL has long denied that its products pose a security risk in the US or anywhere else, or that it’s involved in any way with the military. Zeng often jokes that while batteries are difficult to formulate, they’re “dumb as bricks,” with no use for espionage or any other unintended purpose. But convincing Washington hawks of that may be impossible, since it would essentially require Zeng and his colleagues to prove a negative—and in an environment of suspicion toward all things Chinese.

宁德时代一直否认其产品在美国或任何其他地方构成安全风险，也否认与军方有任何形式的关联。曾毓群常开玩笑说，虽然电池配方复杂，但它们 “ 笨得像砖头 ” ，根本不可能用于间谍活动或其他非预期用途。然而，要想说服华盛顿的强硬派接受这一点，恐怕是徒劳的，因为这本质上要求曾毓群及其团队去证明一件 “ 不存在 ” 的事情 —— 更何况当前环境下，任何与中国相关的事物都备受怀疑。

As a result, Zeng says he’s happy to leave the world’s largest economy well down his priority list for now. Taking a drag on one of his cigarettes, he cites a notion from Sun Tzu’s The Art of War: “Be unconquerable yourself, then wait for the rival to be conquerable.”

因此，曾先生表示，他目前很乐意将全球最大经济体暂时置于其优先事项清单的靠后位置。他吸了一口烟，引用了《孙子兵法》中的理念： “ 先为不可胜，以待敌之可胜。 ”

注： “ 先为不可胜，以待敌之可胜 ” 出自《孙子兵法 · 军形篇》，原文: 孙子曰： “ 昔之善战者，先为不可胜，以待敌之可胜。不可胜在己，可胜在敌。 ” 意思是善战者先巩固自身，使其不被击败（不可胜），然后等待时机，抓住敌人暴露的弱点（可胜）以取胜，核心是 “ 不可胜在己，可胜在敌 ” ，强调先确保自身立于不败之地（防御），再寻求战胜敌人的机会（进攻）。

In the emerging economies that account for an ever greater percentage of automotive demand, such security concerns don’t really figure. For the most part the governments of Brazil, Indonesia, South Africa and other countries are more interested in access to cheap cars than they are in picking a side between the US and China. CATL is betting particularly heavily on Indonesia, because of both the potential for long-term demand for EVs from its almost 300 million people and the panoply of minerals that lie beneath its soil.

在汽车需求占比日益攀升的新兴经济体，上述安全顾虑并非主要考量。无论是巴西、印度尼西亚、南非还是其他国家，其政府大多更关注获取价格低廉的汽车，而非在美国与中国之间选边站队。宁德时代正对印尼市场押下重注，这既源于该国近 3 亿人口带来的电动汽车长期需求潜力，也得益于其地下蕴藏的丰富矿产资源。

CATL recently broke ground on a battery plant in West Java, which will be supplied with nickel and other materials from a sister facility on the island of Halmahera. Together the plants will produce far more than is required to satisfy Indonesian demand, allowing them to supply other CATL outposts around the world, as well as international auto producers.

宁德时代最近在印尼西爪哇省动工建设了一座电池工厂，其所需镍等原材料将由位于哈马黑拉岛的姊妹工厂供应。两座工厂的总产能将远超印尼本土市场需求，因此其产品除了供应 CATL 在全球的其他生产基地外，还将面向国际汽车制造商。

Producing in Indonesia may allow the company to extend its cost advantage as wages rise in China. But it could also create liabilities, especially when it comes to selling into sustainability-minded markets such as Europe. Indonesian battery-materials plants regularly experience nightmarish, fatal accidents, and environmental standards in the country are far lower than in more developed jurisdictions. (CATL says that its operations will “set a strong example of green, circular industrial development” and that it meets strict sustainability standards.)

在印度尼西亚设厂，可能有助于该公司应对中国劳动力成本上升，从而保持成本优势。然而，此举也可能带来风险，尤其是在向欧洲这类注重可持续发展的市场销售产品时。印度尼西亚的电池材料工厂时常发生可怕的致命事故，且该国的环保标准远低于更发达的国家和地区。（宁德时代表示，其运营将 “ 为绿色循环工业发展树立强有力的典范 ” ，并符合严格的可持续发展标准。）

But over time, European and even American carmakers may decide there’s simply no way to avoid importing CATL’s batteries or otherwise partnering with it, not if they plan to offer EVs at a cost competitive with traditional cars. The Trump administration’s dismantling of Biden-era incentives has brought ruthless price competition to the US EV market, where buyers can no longer rely on credits and rebates to defray costs.

但随着时间的推移，欧洲甚至美国的汽车制造商可能会意识到，如果他们想推出价格能与传统燃油车竞争的电动汽车，可能根本无法避免进口宁德时代的电池，或者以其他方式与其合作。特朗普政府废除了拜登时期推出的激励政策，导致美国电动汽车市场陷入激烈的价格战，消费者再也无法依靠税收抵免和补贴来降低购车成本。

The US may also be headed for a glut of domestically produced batteries. South Korea’s LG Energy Solution and SK On Co.—the third- and fifth-largest automotive-battery manufacturers, respectively—have plans on the books to build a total of 10 US plants. (Construction is continuing at one of the highest-profile, a joint venture in Georgia between LG and Hyundai Motor Co., despite the fallout from a major immigration raid on the site.) Japanese companies such as Toyota and Panasonic have likewise sunk billions of dollars into US facilities that may face overcapacity issues before they even start production; one Toyota plant, in North Carolina, began shifting toward hybrid-vehicle batteries only days after it opened.

美国本土生产的电池也可能面临供应过剩的局面。韩国电池制造商 LG 新能源和 SK On—— 分别是全球第三和第五大汽车电池生产商 —— 已计划在美国建设共计 10 家工厂。（其中备受关注的是 LG 与现代汽车在佐治亚州的合资工厂，尽管该厂址曾遭遇大规模移民突袭并引发后续影响，其建设工作仍在持续。）同样，丰田和松下等日本企业也已在美国工厂投入数十亿美元，但这些设施甚至在投产前就可能遭遇产能过剩的问题；例如，丰田位于北卡罗来纳州的一家工厂，在开业仅仅几天后，就开始转向生产混合动力汽车电池。

In August, General Motors Co. shocked the auto industry by announcing plans to purchase made-in-China batteries from CATL for the most affordable EVs it sells in the US.
