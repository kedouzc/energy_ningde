## 一、核心观点 / Key Takeaways

### 1.1 摘要段（首页）

> **EN**
> Despite market concerns over China's weak consumption and soft passenger vehicle sales
> (JPMe FY26 China PV NEV sales -9% y/y vs +6% previously), we believe investors
> **underappreciate the government's latest push in heavy-duty truck (HDT) electrification**.
> China released a Master Plan and targets for HDT electrification on 12 June.
> The government's **40% EV penetration target implies at least ~130GWh of HDT battery
> demand (2025E: 93GWh)**, roughly the size of the US EV market in 2025 and larger than
> the US ESS market. We view the government target as **a floor, not a ceiling**, and expect
> **~50% EV penetration in HDT by 2030**, implying a **>20% CAGR for CV battery demand**
> (vs ~7% for PV demand). We estimate that **China's CV battery demand will reach ~380GWh
> by 2030**, representing **>30% of total China EV battery demand**.

**中文**
尽管市场担忧中国消费疲弱、乘用车销量疲软（JPM 预测 FY26 中国乘用车 NEV 销量同比 **-9%**，
此前预测为 +6%），我们认为投资者**低估了政府最新一轮对重卡电动化的推动**。
中国于 **6 月 12 日**发布重卡电动化总体方案与目标。政府的 **40% 电动化渗透率目标，
意味着重卡电池需求至少约 130GWh（2025 年预计 93GWh）**，
体量大致相当于 2025 年美国电动车市场，并超过美国储能市场。
我们视政府目标为**地板而非天花板**，预计 **2030 年重卡电动化渗透率约 50%**，
对应**商用车电池需求复合增速 >20%**（乘用车约 7%）。
我们测算 **2030 年中国商用车电池需求将达约 380GWh**，占中国电池总需求 **>30%**。

> 📌 **关键校准点**：`~50% by 2030` 与 `~380GWh by 2030` 是原文明确表述，
> 任何还原测算都**不得擅自上调**这两个数。

### 1.2 主要受益者

> **EN**
> **Key beneficiaries: Sinotruk** (OW, leading EV HDT OEM), **CATL** (OW, ~50% share in
> China HDT batteries with best-in-class technology). Among tier-2 battery makers,
> **EVE Energy** (N) has highest CV volume exposure, while **CALB** (OW) is gaining
> meaningful share in HDT battery market (**+5ppts to 10% YTD**).

**中文**
**主要受益者：中国重汽**（增持，领先的电动重卡整车厂）、
**宁德时代**（增持，在中国重卡电池市占约 **50%**，技术业内最优）。
二线电池厂中，**亿纬锂能**（中性）商用车敞口最高；
**中创新航**（增持）在重卡电池市场份额提升显著（**年初至今 +5 个百分点至 10%**）。

### 1.3 政策路线图

> **EN**
> **China's first national HDT electrification roadmap.** China released a Master Plan for
> HDT electrification on 12 June, setting a **40% new-energy penetration target by 2030**.
> The plan also outlines supportive measures to accelerate adoption, including policies to
> expand **charging and battery-swapping infrastructure**. In our view, these initiatives
> formalize a clearer policy runway for HDT electrification.

**中文**
**中国首个国家级重卡电动化路线图。** 中国于 6 月 12 日发布重卡电动化总体方案，
设定 **2030 年新能源渗透率 40%** 的目标。方案还提出加速推广的配套措施，
包括扩大**充电与换电基础设施**的政策。我们认为，这些举措为重卡电动化
确立了更清晰的政策通道。

### 1.4 为什么 40% 是「地板」

> **EN**
> The 40% EV penetration target may not appear aggressive, but **China has a long track
> record of overachieving policy goals**. For instance, the government's 2020 development
> plan targeted passenger NEV penetration of 20% by 2025 and 50% by 2035, yet
> **penetration had already surpassed 50% by 2025**. Similarly, China's renewable energy
> installations have historically **exceeded central government targets by >90% on average**.
> Consistent with this pattern, new-energy HDT penetration rose from **~5% in 2023 to ~25%
> in 2025 and ~29% YTD (with May already reaching ~40%)**, putting the market within
> striking distance of the 40% target. We therefore view the 40% HDT penetration target as
> **a floor, rather than a ceiling**, particularly as improving battery technology, expanding
> charging and battery-swapping infrastructure, and rising uncertainty around LNG prices
> continue to strengthen the economic case for EV HDTs.

**中文**
40% 的渗透率目标看似不激进，但**中国长期有超额完成政策目标的记录**。
例如，2020 年发展规划设定乘用车 NEV 渗透率 2025 年 20%、2035 年 50%，
而**到 2025 年渗透率已超过 50%**。类似地，中国可再生能源装机历史上
**平均超出中央目标 >90%**。与此一致，新能源重卡渗透率已从
**2023 年约 5% 升至 2025 年约 25%，2026 年初至今约 29%（5 月单月已达约 40%）**，
距 40% 目标近在咫尺。因此我们视 40% 为**地板而非天花板**，
尤其在电池技术改善、充换电设施扩张、LNG 价格不确定性上升
持续强化电动重卡经济性的背景下。

> ⚠️ **时间标签务必看清**（此处极易张冠李戴）：
> | 表述 | 对应时间 | 数值 |
> |---|---|---|
> | `~5% in 2023` | 2023 全年 | 5% |
> | `~25% in 2025` | **2025 全年** | **25%** |
> | `~29% YTD` | **2026 年初至今**（对应 Table 1 的 4M26 列） | 29% |
> | `May already reaching ~40%` | 2026 年 5 月单月 | 40% |
>
> **25% 才是 2025 年的渗透率；29% 属于 2026 年**。二者不可互证。

### 1.5 电动化已在放量

> **EN**
> **HDT electrification is already scaling, creating a large and visible battery demand runway.**
> China sold **1.1mn HDTs in 2025 (~780k domestic)**, and EV penetration climbed from
> 5% (2023) to 25% (2025) on improving battery tech and supportive policy. With an
> **installed HDT base of >8mn units** (largely logistics; **~8-10yr life**), replacement demand
> is **~800k+ units p.a.** HDTs are now the key swing factor in China CV battery demand,
> accounting for **~23% of domestic EV battery demand (vs 12% in 2024)**. Under the
> government's 40% new-energy penetration target, domestic HDT battery demand could
> reach at least **~130GWh (vs 93GWh in 2025)**, roughly the size of the US EV market in
> 2025 and larger than the US ESS market.

**中文**
**重卡电动化已在放量，形成清晰可见的电池需求跑道。**
2025 年中国重卡销量 **110 万辆（其中国内约 78 万辆）**，
在电池技术改善与政策支持下，电动化渗透率从 2023 年 5% 升至 2025 年 25%。
重卡**保有量超过 800 万辆**（以物流车为主，**寿命约 8–10 年**），
对应**年更新需求约 80 万辆以上**。重卡已成为中国商用车电池需求的关键摆动因素，
占国内电池需求的 **约 23%（2024 年为 12%）**。在 40% 新能源渗透率目标下，
国内重卡电池需求至少可达 **约 130GWh（2025 年为 93GWh）**，
体量约相当于 2025 年美国电动车市场，并大于美国储能市场。

> 📌 **本段为 7.2 测算的销量假设依据**：
> 保有量 >800 万辆 ÷ 8–10 年寿命 → 年更新需求约 80 万辆，
> 故 7.2 中「国内重卡盘子 78–82 万辆窄幅波动」直接源自此处。

## 二、政策目标 / Master Plan Targets

### 2.1 政策发布主体与框架

> **EN**
> **Implementation plan for scaling up new-energy HDT adoption: China's first national HDT
> electrification roadmap.** China's **Ministry of Transport**, together with **10 other
> government agencies**, including the **National Development and Reform Commission
> (NDRC)**, jointly released the *Implementation Plan for Scaling Up New Energy Heavy-Duty
> Truck Adoption* on **12 June 2026**. This marks the **first national-level policy framework**
> specifically targeting the heavy-duty new-energy sector.

**中文**
**《关于规模化推广新能源重卡的实施方案》：中国首个国家级重卡电动化路线图。**
中国**交通运输部**联合包括**国家发展改革委（NDRC）**在内的**其他 10 个部门**，
于 **2026 年 6 月 12 日**联合发布该实施方案。
这标志着**首个专门针对重型新能源领域的国家级政策框架**。

### 2.2 侧栏：2030 年五大量化目标

原文首页侧栏 *"China's Master Plan in HDT electrification (by 2030)"*：

| # | EN | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 40% new-energy penetration rate | 新能源渗透率 **40%** |
| 2 | 1.6mn new-energy HDTs (20% of HDT parc) | 新能源重卡保有 **160 万辆**（占重卡保有量 **20%**） |
| 3 | 3,000 HDT charging and battery-swapping stations | **3,000 座**重卡充换电站 |
| 4 | 80% electrification of fixed-route short-haul freight transportation in key regions | 重点区域固定线路短途货运电动化率 **80%** |
| 5 | 30,000 km of zero-carbon freight corridors | **3 万公里**零碳货运走廊 |

### 2.3 政策要点逐条

> **EN — Clear industry targets.** The plan targets new-energy HDT sales penetration of
> **40% by 2030**, with the total fleet exceeding **1.6mn vehicles**, representing approximately
> **20% of China's HDT parc**.

**中文 — 明确的行业目标。** 方案目标为 2030 年新能源重卡销量渗透率 **40%**，
保有量超过 **160 万辆**，约占中国重卡保有量的 **20%**。

> **EN — Focus on high-penetration use cases.** The policy targets over **80% electrification
> of fixed-route short-haul freight** transportation in key regions such as **Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei**
> and the **Fenwei Plain** by 2030. These applications, including **mining transportation and
> port logistics**, typically feature short operating distances, high utilization rates and fixed
> routes, making them among the **most economically attractive** use cases for HDT electrification.

**中文 — 聚焦高渗透率场景。** 政策目标为 2030 年在**京津冀**、**汾渭平原**等重点区域，
实现固定线路短途货运电动化率超过 **80%**。这类场景（包括**矿区运输与港口物流**）
通常运距短、利用率高、线路固定，是重卡电动化中**经济性最好**的应用。

> **EN — Accelerating infrastructure deployment.** The plan calls for the construction of
> approximately **3,000 HDT charging and battery-swapping stations** along China's highway
> network, alongside **30,000km of zero-carbon freight corridors**. It also targets increasing
> the share of new-energy HDTs in highway freight transportation to **18%**.

**中文 — 加速基础设施部署。** 方案提出沿中国高速公路网建设约 **3,000 座**
重卡充换电站，以及 **3 万公里**零碳货运走廊，
并将新能源重卡在公路货运中的占比提升至 **18%**。

> **EN — Enhanced financial support.** The plan encourages the use of various funding channels
> to support new-energy HDT purchases and charging/swapping infrastructure deployment.
> It also promotes the use of **local government special bonds** and financing support for both
> HDT operators and infrastructure developers.

**中文 — 强化资金支持。** 方案鼓励运用多种融资渠道支持新能源重卡购置
与充换电设施部署，并推动使用**地方政府专项债**，为重卡运营商与设施开发商提供融资支持。

### 2.4 JPM 的解读

> **EN**
> We believe the significance of this policy **extends beyond the headline target of 40%**
> HDT penetration by 2030. More importantly, it establishes a **comprehensive framework
> covering key industry bottlenecks**, including application scenarios, infrastructure
> deployment, standardization and financing support.
>
> Among the various initiatives, we view **charging and battery-swapping infrastructure
> development as particularly important**. Historically, EV HDT adoption has been
> concentrated in **closed-loop applications such as mines, ports and industrial parks**.
> The proposed construction of 30,000km of zero-carbon freight corridors and 3,000 HDT
> charging and swapping stations could **facilitate broader adoption in long-haul logistics
> markets** and significantly expand the industry's addressable market.

**中文**
我们认为该政策的意义**不止于 40% 这一标题目标**。更重要的是，
它建立了一个**覆盖行业关键瓶颈的完整框架**，
包括应用场景、基础设施部署、标准化与融资支持。

在各项举措中，我们认为**充换电基础设施建设尤为重要**。
历史上电动重卡的应用集中在**矿区、港口、产业园区等闭环场景**。
拟建的 3 万公里零碳货运走廊与 3,000 座重卡充换电站，
有望**推动其在长途物流市场的更广泛应用**，显著扩大行业可触达市场。

> 📌 **对换电赛道的直接含义**：政策把「换电」与「充电」并列写入国家级方案，
> 且明确 3,000 座站的建设目标 —— 这是换电从「企业行为」升级为「国家基建」的标志。

## 五、电池大电量化趋势 / Battery Upsizing

### 5.1 Figure 2：重卡最大续航里程

> **原文标题**：Figure 2: Maximum EV range of HDTs

## 六、TCO 经济性分析 / TCO Analysis

### 6.1 分析框架与结论

> **EN**
> **EV HDTs demonstrate superior TCO economics under current backdrop.**
> We conducted a TCO analysis comparing battery electric heavy-duty trucks (EV HDTs),
> LNG-powered HDTs and diesel HDTs under a typical **high-utilization operating scenario**.
> Our framework incorporates **vehicle purchase costs, energy expenses, maintenance costs,
> government subsidies and payload loss associated with battery weight**.
>
> Key findings include:
> - Despite **higher upfront vehicle purchase costs**, EV HDTs deliver materially lower
>   operating costs due to significantly lower energy and maintenance expenses.
> - Under current subsidy policies and LNG prices, EV HDTs achieve **TCO parity with LNG
>   trucks after approximately three years** of operation.
> - **Diesel HDTs remain the least economical** option across all ownership periods.
>
> Our analysis explicitly incorporates **payload loss** resulting from the heavier battery pack.
> While EV HDTs experience lower effective payload capacity vs LNG and diesel vehicles,
> the fuel savings generated in **high-mileage, high-utilization applications more than offsets**
> the associated revenue loss.

**中文**
**当前环境下电动重卡展现出更优的 TCO 经济性。**
我们在典型的**高利用率运营场景**下，对电动重卡、LNG 重卡与柴油重卡做了 TCO 对比。
框架涵盖**购车成本、能源费用、维保成本、政府补贴，以及电池重量导致的载重损失**。

主要结论：
- 尽管**购车成本更高**，电动重卡凭借显著更低的能源与维保支出，运营成本大幅降低。
- 在当前补贴政策与 LNG 价格下，电动重卡在**运营约三年后与 LNG 车实现 TCO 打平**。
- **柴油重卡在所有持有期内都是最不经济的选项**。

分析明确纳入了因电池更重导致的**载重损失**。
虽然电动重卡有效载重低于 LNG 与柴油车，
但在**高里程、高利用率场景下，燃料节省足以覆盖**相应的收入损失。

## 七、换电专章 / HDT Battery Swap

> ⚠️ **本章是与「宁德时代换电」项目最直接相关的部分**

### 7.1 换电如何改善经济性

> **EN**
> **Battery swap enhances the economics of EV HDTs.** Battery swapping enhances the
> economics of EV HDTs through both **lower upfront investment and higher asset utilization**.
> As charging and swapping infrastructure continues to expand, battery swapping could
> become an increasingly important driver of EV HDT adoption, particularly in
> **high-frequency logistics applications**.

**中文**
**换电改善电动重卡的经济性。** 换电通过**降低前期投入与提升资产利用率**
两条路径改善电动重卡经济性。随着充换电基础设施持续扩张，
换电有望成为电动重卡渗透的日益重要的驱动力，
尤其在**高频物流场景**中。

### 7.2 降低前期投入（BaaS 模式）

> **EN**
> **Battery swapping lowers upfront investment**: Under the **battery-as-a-service (BaaS)**
> model, fleet operators can purchase trucks **without batteries** and lease battery packs
> separately. Given that a typical **400kWh HDT battery pack accounts for approximately
> Rmb300k-400k of vehicle cost**, battery swapping can **reduce initial vehicle purchase
> costs by ~40-50%**, significantly lowering adoption barriers for fleet operators.

**中文**
**换电降低前期投入**：在 **车电分离（BaaS）** 模式下，
车队运营方可购买**不含电池**的车辆，另行租赁电池包。
考虑到典型的 **400kWh 重卡电池包约占整车成本 30–40 万元**，
换电可**将购车初始成本降低约 40–50%**，
显著降低车队运营方的准入门槛。

> 📌 **交叉校验**：Table 3 中电动重卡购车价 70 万元，
> 若扣除 30–40 万元电池，裸车约 30–40 万元，
> 与 LNG 车 45 万元、柴油车 40 万元处于同一区间甚至更低——
> **这正是 BaaS 模式解决「购车贵」痛点的量化依据**。

### 7.3 提升车队利用率

> **EN**
> **Battery swapping improves fleet utilization**: Battery swapping reduces energy
> replenishment time to **approximately five minutes**, comparable to conventional charging
> at **~1.5 hours**. Compared to traditional charging solutions, a battery-swapping HDT
> operating **150,000km annually can gain approximately 200 additional operating hours
> per year**, translating into roughly **Rmb40k of incremental annual revenue per vehicle**
> through higher utilization rates and improved operational efficiency.

**中文**
**换电提升车队利用率**：换电将补能时间缩短至**约 5 分钟**，
而常规充电约需 **1.5 小时**。
相比传统充电方案，年行驶 **15 万公里**的换电重卡
**每年可多获得约 200 个运营小时**，
通过更高的利用率与运营效率，折算为**单车年增收约 4 万元**。

> 📌 **这是换电价值量化的关键数据**：
> **单车年增收 4 万元** —— 可直接用于测算换电服务的付费意愿上限。

### 7.4 换电基础设施现状

> **EN**
> According to data from the **China Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Promotion
> Alliance (EVCIPA)**, China had **6,039 battery swapping stations** in operation as of
> **April 2026**. Approximately **1,000 new stations were added during 4M26**, representing
> **over 40% YoY growth**. While official statistics do not separately disclose HDT battery
> swapping stations, **J.P. Morgan estimates there are currently 700-1,200 HDT battery
> swapping stations nationwide, accounting for 12-20% of total** battery swapping infrastructure.
>
> Although the HDT battery swapping network remains at an **early stage**, policy support is
> increasing significantly. Under the recently announced Implementation Plan, China targets
> approximately **3,000 HDT charging and battery swapping stations** and 30,000km of
> zero-carbon freight corridors by 2030. Relative to the current installed base, we believe the
